Whiskers & Wags New-Client Checklist
Love and care when you're not there!

Before your first confirmed visit:

[ ] Submit the simple request form and wait for availability confirmation.
[ ] Complete the private client intake form after approval.
[ ] Confirm your care address and best contact number.
[ ] Share emergency contact details.
[ ] Share veterinarian information and emergency vet preference.
[ ] Write feeding amounts, food locations, treat rules, and bowl locations.
[ ] List medications, doses, timing, allergies, mobility needs, or anxiety notes.
[ ] Share walk routines, leash/harness locations, litter box details, favorite toys, and comfort routines.
[ ] Confirm home access instructions, parking, alarm/key/gate details, and preferred backup plan.
[ ] Note mail, plant, trash, lights, thermostat, or home check-in tasks.
[ ] Place supplies where they are easy to find before the first visit.
[ ] Send any last-minute updates before you leave.

Tip: Do not send sensitive access codes in a public message. Use the private intake form only after approval, or share access details directly by phone if preferred.
