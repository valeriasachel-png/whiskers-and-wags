Do you offer overnight sitting?

Overnight and extended care requests are reviewed individually. Send your dates and care needs so we can confirm what is possible.

Do you give medication?

Medication reminders and special care routines may be supported with clear written instructions. Detailed medical needs are collected privately after availability is confirmed.

What areas do you serve?

Whiskers & Wags serves Madison, Flowood, Ridgeland, Gluckstadt, and Northeast Jackson, Mississippi. Nearby requests can use “Other / Ask Us.”

How far ahead should I book?

Request care as early as possible, especially around holidays, school breaks, and busy travel weekends. Requests are not booked until personally confirmed.
