Privacy Policy

How Whiskers & Wags handles information shared through this website.

Effective date: May 24, 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit an initial pet sitting request, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, preferred reply method, requested service dates, service area, pet basics, and a brief needs note. If availability is confirmed, we may later request additional care details such as care address, emergency contact, veterinarian information, home access instructions, feeding routines, medications, and pet care notes.

How We Use Information

We use request information to respond to your inquiry, confirm availability, understand care needs, and arrange requested pet or home sitting services.

Sharing and Storage

Request details are sent through our email delivery provider to the Whiskers & Wags business inbox. We do not sell customer information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested services, comply with law, or protect safety.

Basic Website Analytics

The website may record simple conversion events, such as clicks on request buttons, phone links, email links, downloads, and Amazon affiliate buttons. These events are used to understand which parts of the site are helpful and do not include private form answers.

Affiliate Links

Our Pet Care Picks page may contain affiliate links. If active Amazon affiliate links are used, the page displays the applicable disclosure and qualifying purchases may earn us a commission.

Your Choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information submitted through the request form by contacting us at whiskersandwags811@gmail.com.
